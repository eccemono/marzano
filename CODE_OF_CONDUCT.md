# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and specific in technical disagreement.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Respect that maintainers may decline a change that does not fit the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces - issues, pull requests, discussions and
commit messages - and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report abusive behaviour to the maintainer, `@eccemono`, privately. Reports are
reviewed and handled as confidentially as possible. Maintainers may remove
comments, close contributions, or block accounts as needed.
